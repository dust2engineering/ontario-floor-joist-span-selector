Basis

Table 9.23.4.2.-A applies where floors serve residential areas (Table 4.1.5.3 live loads). The joists must also pass the vibration criterion of 9.23.4.2.(2) — a span that satisfies this table can still feel bouncy if vibration governs. Note how restraint buys span: bridging and strapping restrain the joist edges, which is why the same 38×184 SPF joist grows from 3.54 m (strapping, 300 mm) to 3.99 m (strapping + bridging).
